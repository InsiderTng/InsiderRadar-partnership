01 / MARKET BIAS

Assess Market Direction

Evaluate the direction the market may be favouring. InsiderRadar provides market-bias information to help traders assess potential bullish or bearish conditions across supported currency pairs and timeframes.

02 / MOMENTUM

Evaluate Market Momentum

Assess directional momentum and buying or selling pressure to gain additional context about current market conditions and the strength of a potential move.

Use additional analytical information to help evaluate potential confirmation before making a trading decision. InsiderRadar is designed to complement your strategy, not replace your judgement.
